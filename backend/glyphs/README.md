The four Roblox private-use glyphs (U+E000 Verified, U+E001 Premium,
U+E002 Robux, U+E003 Roblox Plus) are rasterized from Roblox Studio's
RobloxEmoji.ttf. Roblox owns this artwork.

emoji-index.json contains sequence and image-name metadata from iamcal/emoji-data
16.0.0, https://github.com/iamcal/emoji-data. The metadata is MIT licensed;
see emoji-data-LICENSE.txt. Emoji artwork is downloaded on demand from the
matching version of emoji-datasource-apple, -google, -facebook or -twitter.
The vendor artwork retains its respective ownership and licenses, described at
https://github.com/iamcal/emoji-data#licence.
